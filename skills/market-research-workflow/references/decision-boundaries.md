# Decision boundaries

The skill may organize evidence, identify alternatives, explain governing considerations, and record the authorized user's decision. It may not originate:

- commercial product or commercial service determination;
- set-aside or socioeconomic program decision;
- contract-type decision;
- competition strategy;
- consolidation or bundling determination;
- prospective source responsibility or capability determination;
- price-reasonableness determination;
- final acquisition strategy.

Historical shares or percentages are descriptive. Do not convert them into automatic thresholds.

For FAR 19.502-2, separate the legal standard from the evidence. Historical small-business awards may identify firms or support outreach, but the Contracting Officer must have a reasonable expectation that offers will be obtained from at least two responsible small-business concerns and that award will be made at fair market prices. Capability, quality, delivery, and market-price evidence matter.

For sole-source or limited-competition documents, treat the document as evidence of an asserted basis. Independently verify current facts and do not assume approval, continuing validity, or applicability to the new action.

Present each reserved decision as: decision owner, evidence for, evidence against or uncertainty, options, unresolved facts, and the user's approved conclusion.
